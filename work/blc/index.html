The Situation

No pipeline.
No system.

BLC helps businesses recover tax credits. New business came from referrals and inbound only. No outbound motion. No sequences. No prospecting process.

When referrals slowed down, revenue slowed down. They needed pipeline that didn't depend on who happened to call that month.

They didn't want to hire a sales team. They wanted a system one person could run.

What Was Built

Public records.
Not bought lists.

The system found businesses entering situations where they'd need BLC's help — a new filing, a policy change, a regulatory event. Not a bought list of companies that looked right. Companies where something specific was happening right now.

Every first message referenced a fact about the prospect's business. That's why they replied — the message told them something true about their own situation that they didn't expect a stranger to know.

12% reply rate. Average for cold outreach is 1-3%.

Total tool cost: $400/month. One person ran the entire operation.

Three Years

The system
compounded.

Same person. Same stack. The longer it ran, the sharper the targeting got. Feedback from every call refined what situations converted and which ones didn't.

Months 1-3
First conversations

Months 4-12
Pipeline compounds

Every call refined what worked. Which situations led to meetings, which didn't. Reply rates stabilized at 12%. Pipeline grew consistently without adding headcount or tools.

Year 2-3
$5.2M pipeline

The system ran itself. Same process, same person, same $400/month stack. $5.2M in total pipeline. $1M closed-won. No SDR team. No agency. No ramp time for new hires.

Why It Worked
Every message started with a fact, not a pitch. The prospect read something true about their own company that they didn't expect a stranger to know. That's why they replied.

You know the difference between calling someone cold and calling someone whose situation you already understand. When you know what's happening at their company before you dial, the first 10 seconds sound completely different. The system made sure every call and every email started with something specific and verifiable. Not flattery. Not a guess. A fact from the public record about their business, timed to when it was relevant.
