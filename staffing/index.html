What the system watches

Six signals that a facility
needs your call.

All from CMS federal data. Staffing and turnover are drawn from quarterly Payroll-Based Journal submissions; other measures follow their own CMS cadence. Not a bought list. Not a guess. Every facility scored on what's actually happening inside.

01
Low staffing rating

CMS rates every nursing home 1-5 on staffing. A 1 or 2 means they're running short. These facilities are the most likely to pick up the phone.

02
High nurse turnover

60%+ nursing staff turnover means they can't keep people. They're either burning through agency staff already or about to start. Either way, they need options.

03
RN hours below threshold

When RN hours per resident per day drop below 0.5, the facility may be stretched thin on its most critical role. Worth a conversation.

04
Health deficiencies

10+ deficiencies on the most recent CMS survey. Many are staffing-related. A facility under scrutiny from regulators is more motivated to fix coverage gaps fast.

05
Ownership change

New ownership in the last 12 months. New owners clean house, restructure staff, and bring in outside help. The transition period is when they need agencies most.

06
Low overall rating

A 1 or 2 star overall CMS rating. These facilities are under pressure from families, regulators, and corporate. Staffing is almost always part of the fix.

How it works

Former clients first.
New facilities second.

The highest-conversion calls on the list are facilities that already know you. The system finds them first, then adds new facilities in your market showing staffing pressure. No dashboard to learn. No tool to log into. A ranked list with the phone number and what to say.

  • Your old clients come back first Send your past client list. The system cross-references it against CMS data and flags former facilities that are struggling again — high turnover, low ratings, new deficiencies. Warm outreach to people who already know you.
  • New facilities fill the pipeline Federal staffing ratings, turnover, deficiency counts, RN hours, ownership changes. Every facility in your market scored and ranked by urgency. Every list shows a "data as of" date so you know exactly what you're working with.
  • You call with a reason Not "just checking in." You know their staffing rating, their turnover number, their deficiency count. The administrator hears something useful about their own facility — information that helps them whether they hire you or not. That's a different conversation.
  • The scoring gets smarter Feedback loop: which facilities answered, which converted, which were dead ends. Your outreach data improves the scoring. Over time, the list reflects what a good account looks like for your agency — not a generic database.
  • The list refreshes weekly Monitored weekly; refreshed whenever CMS releases new data. Situations change. New facilities enter crisis. Former clients slip back into trouble. The system catches all of it. Monday morning, new list.
