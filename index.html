The Situation

When hiring slows down, staffing owners feel it first. The job orders dry up. The phone stops ringing. The instinct is to chase new logos.

But the fastest revenue isn't new business. It's the clients who already know your firm, already approved your rates, already signed your agreements. They stopped ordering six months ago. Their last job order expired. They went with a competitor on one deal and nobody followed up.

Those clients are still hiring. Some of them are posting roles right now that look exactly like the ones your team filled before. They just haven't heard from you.

The Job-Posting Match

Past clients, current openings.

For example: A logistics company placed 3 welders with your firm in 2024. This week, they posted 4 welding roles on Indeed. That's not a cold call. That's a reason to reach out, with something specific to say.

When there's a match, that client moves to the top of the outreach list with the posting details attached.
