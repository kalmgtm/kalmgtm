How It Works

Start small.
Prove it. Then scale.

I don't start with a six-month roadmap. I start with a 30-day outbound test — one audience, one message, real execution. You'll have the evidence to decide what's next before committing to anything larger.

01
⬩
Test

Thirty days. One audience, one message, up to 100 accounts. I research the accounts, develop the messaging, and run an approved campaign. Your team receives any qualified responses, a full record of what happened, and a clear recommendation: continue, revise, or stop.

When the test supports expansion, I build the repeatable system around the findings — account scoring, enrichment, targeting logic, outreach sequences. Your team gets a weekly list of prioritized accounts with a specific reason to reach out to each one.
