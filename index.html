How It Works

Closed deals first.
Then scale.

Your best deals contain the evidence of what good looks like. We extract it, validate it, and build the workflow around it.

01
⬡
Study

We study the customer relationships you most want to repeat and interview the people who won and serve those accounts. What was true about the account before they chose you? What conditions existed? We test those patterns against your losses to separate signal from coincidence.

02
◈
Build

The patterns become a scoring model. Public data, your account history, and enrichment sources are connected into a workflow that surfaces accounts matching your winning conditions — ranked by how closely they match, and what has changed recently.

03
◎
Learn

As your team acts on the recommendations, we capture what happened: called, conversation, qualified, won, dead end. The model improves from real outcomes — not open rates. The longer it runs, the sharper it gets.

A data tool gives everyone the same alerts. It doesn't know which conditions preceded your best deals, which account types your team actually converts, or what changed since your last engagement with a former client. The model we build starts from your evidence — not a generic database.
Weeks 5-8, your team receives weekly ranked account lists — but doesn't act on them yet. Instead, they tell us: would you call this account? Is the timing right? Is this the right contact?
